# cities-stars-and-dragons
## …and other things Ethereum named its upgrades

This book tells the stories of how Ethereum upgrade names came to be.
It is based on public information from Ethereum Improvement Proposals (EIPs),
core developer meetings, official announcements and community discussions,
along with my own experiences and memories from years of helping coordinate
Ethereum upgrades.
It is written in a simple and friendly way, with illustrations, so that people of all
ages can enjoy it. This book is for anyone who is curious about Ethereum,
whether you are new to the ecosystem, a long-time contributor, or someone who
just likes a good story.
Upgrade names are more than just labels. They are part of Ethereum’s journey
and reflect the people, ideas, and conversations that shape it.
I hope this book inspires you to imagine what comes next, and to be part of
Ethereum’s future.
